# SEC-20 Privacy and Customer Data Processing

## Processing

20.1 Halcyon Ridge Software processes customer personal data only on documented customer instructions under a data processing agreement.

20.2 Customer data is not used to train machine learning models.

20.3 Customer data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

## Customer rights

20.4 Customers can export their data in a machine-readable format at any time during the contract.

20.5 Data subject requests received by Halcyon Ridge Software are forwarded to the customer within 5 business days.
