# SEC-16 Endpoint Security

## Company devices

16.1 Company laptops are centrally managed, and full-disk encryption is enforced.

16.2 Endpoint detection and response software runs on all company laptops.

16.3 Operating system security updates are applied within 14 days of release.

## Personal devices

16.4 Personal devices may reach email and chat through a managed app but may not access production systems or source code.
