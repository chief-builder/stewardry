# SEC-18 Personnel Security and Training

## Hiring

18.1 Background checks are performed on all employees before they start, where local law permits.

18.2 Employees and contractors sign confidentiality agreements before they receive access.

## Training

18.3 All workforce members complete security awareness training at hire and every year.

18.4 Phishing simulations are run at least every quarter.
