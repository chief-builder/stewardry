# SEC-14 Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

## Resilience

14.1 The production service is deployed across at least two availability zones in each hosting region.

14.2 The recovery time objective for the production service is 8 hours, and the recovery point objective is 1 hour.

## Testing and continuity

14.3 Disaster recovery procedures, including a restore from backup, are tested at least annually.

14.4 A business continuity plan covers the loss of office facilities and of key personnel.
