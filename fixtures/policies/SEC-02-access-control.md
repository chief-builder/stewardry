# SEC-02 Access Control

## Granting access

2.1 Access to production systems and customer data follows least privilege and is granted by role.

2.2 Access requests require approval by the resource owner and are recorded in the ticketing system.

## Reviewing and removing access

2.3 System owners review user access to production every quarter, and inappropriate access is removed within 5 business days.

2.4 Access is revoked within 24 hours of a workforce member's termination.

2.5 Shared accounts are prohibited, except documented break-glass accounts, which are vaulted and monitored.

2.6 Support staff do not access customer data without a support request approved by the customer.
