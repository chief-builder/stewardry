# SEC-13 Vendor Risk Management

## Assessment

13.1 Vendors that process customer data are assessed before onboarding and every year after that.

13.2 Vendors that process customer data must sign a data processing agreement.

13.3 Vendor access to Halcyon Ridge Software systems is time-limited and reviewed every quarter.

## Subprocessors and hosting

13.4 The list of subprocessors is published to customers, and customers are notified 30 days before a new subprocessor is added.

13.5 Halcyon Ridge Software hosts its production environment with a single major cloud provider, in regions in the United States and the European Union.
