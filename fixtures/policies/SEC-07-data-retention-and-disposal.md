# SEC-07 Data Retention and Disposal

## Retention

7.1 Customer data is retained for the duration of the contract and deleted within 30 days after the contract ends, unless the customer requests earlier deletion.

7.2 Backups are retained for 35 days and then deleted automatically.

7.3 Audit logs are retained for 1 year.

## Disposal

7.4 On request, deletion of customer data from primary systems is confirmed to the customer in writing.

7.5 Decommissioned storage is sanitized through the cloud provider's media sanitization process. Halcyon Ridge Software operates no physical storage media.
