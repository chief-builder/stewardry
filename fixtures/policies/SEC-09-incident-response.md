# SEC-09 Incident Response

## Preparation

9.1 Halcyon Ridge Software maintains an incident response plan and tests it at least annually with a tabletop exercise.

9.2 The incident commander may engage external forensic support under a pre-arranged retainer.

## Handling and notification

9.3 Security incidents are classified by severity within 1 hour of detection.

9.4 Affected customers are notified of a confirmed security incident involving their data within 72 hours of confirmation.

9.5 Every severity 1 or severity 2 incident receives a written post-incident review within 10 business days.
