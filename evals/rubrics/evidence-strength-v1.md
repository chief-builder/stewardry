# Evidence strength rubric, version 1 (spec section 5.1)

Qualitative labels for how well current, accessible policy passages support
a drafted answer. They are **not** calibrated probabilities. Numerical
calibration is later scope.

| Label | Meaning | Allowed next step |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct current support** | One or more current, accessible passages state the answer plainly. No current passage contradicts it | May proceed to review and approval |
| **Partial or ambiguous support** | Passages cover only part of the question, or need interpretation (for example, employees are covered but contractors are not) | Routed to an expert; not approvable as is |
| **Insufficient, conflicting, or stale support** | No passage covers the question; current passages disagree; or the only evidence is superseded or past its review date | Routed to an expert; not approvable until an authoritative source is fixed |

A superseded passage never counts as support. A confidential passage counts
only for a reader who may access it.
